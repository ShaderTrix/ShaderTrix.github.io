My Techart Stuff

Hey, I'm ShaderTrix and this is my techart stuff — a mix of systems I've shipped on production mobile games and personal projects I built just to learn and mess around with. Some of these were built for real teams with millions of players, others were weekend experiments that got a bit out of hand. If anything here interests you or you want to chat about how something works, feel free to reach out.

Some of these also come with write-ups or tutorials breaking down how they work, others don't yet — I'm slowly adding them as I find the time, so check back if something looks interesting but is missing the deep-dive.
